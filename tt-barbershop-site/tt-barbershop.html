The shop

Regulars come back
for a reason

TT Barber Shop is a local barbers on Milleara Road run by Tom, the kind of barber who remembers how you like it before you sit down. Fresh cuts, clean fades, beard trims done properly — no cattle-run, no rushing you out the door.

Kids get patience and a steady hand. Regulars get a lollipop and a glass of water while they wait. And if your dog needs to come in out of the heat, that's fine too — George the resident good boy will vouch for it.
